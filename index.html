Purpose of the app

Built for our
channel owner.

Riddle Wonders Automation is a privately operated production tool for RiddleWonders. It supports quiz preparation, Adam voice narration through ElevenLabs, review of video assets, and approved YouTube uploads.

Only the authorized channel owner connects a Google account. The app is not offered as a public service for connecting other people’s channels.

About this website

These pages explain the app and its data practices. This is an information website, not a sign-in or upload portal.

Google authorization

Clear permission.
A specific purpose.

The app requests YouTube upload access so it can send approved videos to the owner’s channel.

See how Google data is used
What is sent to YouTube
The approved video and its title, description, tags, category, visibility, and audience setting.
Why upload records are kept
Upload outcomes and video IDs help prevent repeat uploads and investigate failures.
How the owner stays in control
The owner authorizes Google access, approves uploads, and can revoke the app’s connection in Google Account settings.
