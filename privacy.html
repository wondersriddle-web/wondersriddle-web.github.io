About this policy

This policy covers Riddle Wonders Automation, an owner-operated tool for the RiddleWonders YouTube channel, and this information website. The app uses YouTube API Services. It prepares quiz content and supports uploads that the channel owner has approved. Google authorization is intended only for the authorized channel owner; this is not a public channel-management service.

The operator can be contacted at wondersriddle@gmail.com.

Google access and information processed

The app requests the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload approved videos to YouTube. Google handles account sign-in and consent. The app does not ask for the owner’s Google account password.

For the upload workflow, the app processes:

  • Authorization information: the OAuth client configuration, the owner’s refresh token, and access tokens used to authenticate requests to Google.
  • Approved content: video files, titles, descriptions, tags, video category, visibility, and the audience setting supplied with an upload.
  • Operational information: upload status, YouTube video IDs and URLs, and workflow or reservation identifiers used to trace an upload and prevent duplication.

The implemented upload workflow does not request access to Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Drive, subscriber lists, or YouTube Analytics.

How information is used

Authorization information enables the app to authenticate the channel owner’s approved uploads. Video files and their metadata are sent to YouTube for publication with the selected settings. Operational records help prevent repeat uploads, reconcile interrupted attempts, and investigate errors.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. Our policy is to use information received from Google APIs only for the purposes described here and in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Services involved in the workflow

Google and YouTube
Google authenticates the owner. YouTube receives the approved video and metadata and returns upload information. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
GitHub
GitHub Actions runs the private automation workflow. OAuth client information and the refresh token are supplied through private Actions secrets. Generated media, review artifacts, operational records, and run logs may be held in the private workflow environment. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
ElevenLabs
Narration text is sent separately to ElevenLabs to generate the approved Adam voice. This narration request uses a separate ElevenLabs credential; the implemented request does not send Google authorization credentials or YouTube upload records to ElevenLabs. See the ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.

Each provider also handles information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not authorize these providers to use Google user data for unrelated advertising or model training.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are maintained in the private automation environment for as long as the connection is needed, unless they are revoked or removed. Access tokens are obtained during workflow execution. Generated media and review files may be retained as private workflow artifacts.

Upload identifiers and history are kept as needed to prevent duplicates and resolve uncertain upload outcomes. Records may remain in private repository history and workflow logs. Artifact and log retention depends on the relevant workflow and provider settings, so there is no single retention period for every item.

The operator controls access to the private automation environment. Deletion requests are handled manually; this app does not provide an automatic deletion service or promise automatic removal from all historical records.

Revoke access or request deletion

The channel owner can review or revoke the app’s access through Google Account third-party connections or the Google permissions page. Revocation prevents the app from continuing to use the revoked authorization.

Revoking access does not itself delete already published YouTube videos, stored media, or operational history. Published content can be managed in YouTube Studio. To request removal of app-managed authorization credentials or other retained information, email wondersriddle@gmail.com. The operator will review the request, verify the requester’s authority, and explain the action taken or any records that need to be retained.

Questions about a specific upload?

Include the video URL or approximate upload date so the operator can identify the relevant record.

Visitors to this information website

This website has no app-added analytics, advertising, forms, scripts, or cookies. It does not connect visitors’ Google accounts or accept video uploads. When this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. External links take you to services with their own policies.

If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your question or request.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact wondersriddle@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
