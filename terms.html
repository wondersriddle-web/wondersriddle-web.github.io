Authorized use

Riddle Wonders Automation is an owner-operated tool for creating RiddleWonders quiz content and uploading approved videos to the authorized YouTube channel. Access to its Google authorization and publishing workflow is restricted to the channel owner or a person explicitly authorized by that owner.

This public website provides information about the app. It does not offer account registration, Google sign-in, or a public upload service.

Content and upload approval

The channel owner reviews the finished video and approves each upload. The operator is responsible for having the necessary rights to the content, choosing accurate metadata and audience settings, and following the policies of the services used.

Do not use the app to access an account without permission, submit unauthorized content, bypass upload safeguards, or repeat uploads that have already been completed.

YouTube API use

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app’s YouTube functionality, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Use must also comply with the applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

YouTube controls how uploaded content is processed, displayed, restricted, or removed on its platform.

Privacy and authorization

Our Privacy Policy explains the information processed by the app, the services involved, retention, and how to contact the operator about deletion.

The channel owner may revoke Google access through Google Account settings. Revocation does not automatically remove videos already uploaded to YouTube.

Workflow availability

The app depends on Google, YouTube, GitHub, and ElevenLabs. Authorization changes, service availability, quotas, and content restrictions may interrupt a workflow. If an upload outcome is uncertain, the operator checks the existing upload record and channel before attempting it again.

Contact and updates

For questions about the app or these terms, email wondersriddle@gmail.com. These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the app. The effective date above identifies the current version.
